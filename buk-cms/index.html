Quality Healthcare Services for BUK Students

Welcome to the Bayero University Kano (New Campus) Digital Clinic System. Providing fast, paperless, and secure medical care and record management for all students.

Fast & Efficient Care

Seamless workflow from Reception to Nurse Triage, Doctor Consultation, Lab Tests, X-ray, and Pharmacy dispensing.

How a visit works

1

Register

Find the student by Registration number and open the file.

2

Triage

The nurse records vital signs. Emergencies go first.

3

Consultation

The doctor sees the file, then requests a test or prescribes.

4

Pharmacy or Diagnostics

Results go back to the doctor. Drugs are dispensed and stock updates.
