YOUR HEALTHCARE JOURNEY

How a Clinic Visit Works

From registration to treatment, BUK Care helps organise each stage of your healthcare journey at Bayero University Kano, New Campus.

01
First Step

Registration & Check-in

The Records Officer verifies the patient's clinic information, opens the medical file and creates a visit record.

Patient enters the clinic queue.

02
Nurse Station

Nurse Triage

The nurse assesses the patient, records vital signs and determines the urgency of the patient's condition.

Emergency cases receive priority.

03
Doctor Station

Doctor Consultation

The doctor reviews the patient's medical information, assesses the complaint, records diagnosis and prepares a treatment plan.

Tests or prescriptions may be requested.

04
Treatment

Diagnostics & Pharmacy

Requested laboratory or X-ray tests are completed and results return to the doctor. Prescribed medicines and medical supplies are then dispensed by the pharmacist.

Visit is completed after treatment.

Important Information

Not every patient requires laboratory or X-ray investigations. These services are provided when requested by the doctor based on the patient's clinical needs.
